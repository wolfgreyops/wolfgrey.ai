      The AI Transition Package

      Easy for AI agents to find, understand and act on.

      One fixed-scope engagement. You keep the site you have. We rebuild what sits underneath it so that search engines, answer engines and agents can all use it.

      Your site was built for people who browse. Agents do not browse. They fetch a page, look for facts they can trust, and try to complete a task: get a price, check availability, book, order. If any of those steps fails, the agent moves to a competitor whose site works, and the customer never sees your name. We close that gap.

      1. 01

        Find

        SEO
        • Fix crawl and index problems
        • Page speed, titles, internal links and sitemap
        • Robots rules that let search and AI crawlers in on purpose, not block them by a default
        What it changes

        Search engines and AI crawlers can reach every page that matters.

      2. 02

        Understand

        AEO
        • Structured data for the business, services, products, prices, hours, service area, reviews and FAQs
        • Key pages rewritten to answer the questions customers ask, in plain statements an assistant can quote
        • Name, address, phone and offer details that match everywhere they appear
        What it changes

        Answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI results can state what you do, where, and for how much, and cite you for it.

      3. 03

        Act

        Agent-ready
        • Quote, booking, contact and order forms with labeled fields and no dead ends
        • No bot wall in front of a paying customer's agent
        • A machine-readable summary of the business and, where you sell products, an agent-ready product feed
        What it changes

        An agent sent to get a quote, book a slot or place an order can finish the job on your site.
