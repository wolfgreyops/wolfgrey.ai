    Questions

    What's the difference between SEO and AEO?

    SEO is how you rank in search results. AEO, answer engine optimization, is how you get named inside AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews and others. Both reward the same thing: clear, specific, trustworthy answers to the questions your customers actually ask. We build for both at once.

    Will AI-written content hurt our Google ranking?

    Google's guidance is about whether content is helpful and accurate, not how it was produced. Every page we publish starts from your real expertise, is reviewed by us, and goes live only after you approve it.

    Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

    Yes, if you want them to, and most owners do. The agent introduces itself as your assistant, writes in your voice, handles the first reply, the questions and the booking, and passes anything sensitive to a person right away.

    What happens when someone leaves a bad review?

    You hear about it first. The agent alerts you and drafts a calm, specific reply for you to approve. Because every job gets a follow-up, unhappy customers are often caught earlier, before they ever post.

    Do we need to be technical?

    No. We do the technical work and explain everything in plain language. Your team just sees more booked jobs and fewer missed calls.

    What does it cost?

    It depends on scope. Most engagements are a one-time build plus an optional monthly plan to keep the agents running and improving. You'll get a clear quote after our first call.
