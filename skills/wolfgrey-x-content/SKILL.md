## Brand Voice (Quick Reference)

- Direct, operator-to-operator tone
- Results-first, no fluff
- Specific numbers over vague claims

```

The real unlock: treating it like a junior employee.

Assign tasks. Review work. Give feedback.

That's when it starts saving hours.
```

## Writing Rules

1. **Hook in first line** — Stop the scroll
2. **One idea per tweet** — Don't cram
3. **Whitespace matters** — Use line breaks
4. **Numbers are specific** — "10 hours" not "hours"
5. **End with action** — Tell them what to do next
6. **No hashtags** — They look spammy
7. **Minimal emojis** — One per tweet max, if any

## Formatting Tips

- Use line breaks liberally
- Bold claims at the start
- Lists work well (use → or •)
- Keep sentences short
- End threads with clear CTA

## Topics That Perform

### High Engagement
- "Here's what I learned building X"
- Specific time/money saved
- Contrarian takes on AI
- Step-by-step workflows
- Before/after comparisons
- Mistakes to avoid

### Lower Engagement
- Generic AI news
- Product announcements without value
- Abstract concepts without examples
- Anything that sounds like an ad
