# wolfgrey Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

**wolfgrey.ai** helps small businesses implement AI systems that save time, cut costs, and scale operations.

**Tagline:** Practical AI systems and ready-to-use workflows for small businesses.

## Voice & Tone

### Core Principles
- **Direct, operator-to-operator** — Write like you're talking to a smart business owner
- **Results-first, no fluff** — Lead with outcomes, skip the hype
- **Specific over vague** — Use real numbers, concrete examples
- **Conversational but professional** — Friendly without being casual

### Writing Style
- Short sentences. Short paragraphs.
- Active voice always
- Second person ("you") when addressing the audience
- First person plural ("we") when representing wolfgrey

### Prohibited Phrases
Never use:
- "Revolutionary" / "Game-changing"
- "Unlock the power of..."
- "Leverage AI to..."
- "Transform your business"
- "Cutting-edge" / "State-of-the-art"
- "Synergy" / "Paradigm shift"
- Generic AI hype language

### Preferred Phrases
Use instead:
- "Save X hours per week"
- "Cut costs by Y%"
- "Here's how it works"
- "Real example:"
- "The result:"
- "Try this:"
