## POST 8 - AaaS: Deploying an Agent

**[AaaS] 🧵**

"Deploying an agent" sounds technical.

In practice, it's three things:

**1/3**

*Characters: 80*

---

1. Context: What does this agent need to know? Your processes, your examples, your constraints.

2. Scope: What decisions can it make alone? What needs your approval?

3. Triggers: When does it run? On a schedule? When data changes? When you ask?

**2/3**

*Characters: 277*

---

That's it. Context, scope, triggers.

The technical setup matters, but the thinking is what most businesses skip.

Get clear on those three, and deployment is just configuration.

## POST 10 - Framework: The 4Ds

**[Framework] 🧵**

Anthropic's 4D framework for working with AI agents:

Delegation. Description. Discernment. Diligence.

What each one means for your business:

**1/4**

*Characters: 175*

---

Delegation: What tasks are you handing off? Be specific. "Help with marketing" fails. "Draft weekly LinkedIn posts based on our content calendar" works.

**2/4**

*Characters: 187*

---

Description: How well can you explain what good looks like? Agents need examples, context, constraints. The clearer your description, the less you edit outputs.

**3/4**

*Characters: 192*

---

Discernment: Where does the agent need judgment? Define the boundaries. What can it decide alone? What requires your review? Discernment gaps create problems.

Diligence: Verify outputs. Trust but check. Especially early.

**4/4**

*Characters: 261*

## POST 12 - Framework: Prompting Techniques

**[Framework] 🧵**

Six prompting techniques that actually move the needle:

**1/3**

*Characters: 71*

---

3. Constraints: Boundaries and rules

These three turn vague outputs into usable ones.

**2/3**

*Characters: 183*

---

5. "Think first": Ask for reasoning before answers
