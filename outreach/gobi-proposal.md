## The Problem

Glenn is spending 5+ hours a day on lead gen tasks that follow the same pattern every time. Morgan is repeating himself across teams and manually tracking orders in spreadsheets. Leads go cold between steps because follow-up is manual, and nothing talks to anything else.

Here's what we'd build to fix that.

---

## Phase 1: Lead Engine

**What it solves:** Glenn's 5-hour daily grind of sourcing leads, writing cold emails, and managing outreach manually across UpLead, LinkedIn, and Mailchimp.

**What gets built:**

- Automated lead enrichment pipeline: new UpLead contacts get formatted, scored, and pushed into your CRM with zero manual entry
- AI-assisted cold email drafts generated from prospect data (industry, role, company size) -- Glenn reviews and sends, not writes from scratch
- LinkedIn post scheduling system with a content template library so posts go out consistently without daily effort
- Mailchimp campaign triggers tied to CRM stages so follow-up emails fire automatically when leads hit specific milestones

**Time saved:** ~3 hours/day for Glenn

---

## Phase 2: Pipeline Autopilot

**What it solves:** Leads going cold after sample requests. No-shows on scheduling. The "then nothing happens" problem.

**What gets built:**

- Automated follow-up sequences at every pipeline stage: post-contact-form, post-intro, post-sample-ship, post-pricing
- Smart scheduling integration: when a prospect fills out the contact form, they get a calendar link immediately -- no email tag required
- Sample shipment tracking with automated check-ins ("Your sample shipped Monday -- any questions?") timed to delivery windows
- CRM stage automation: leads move through pipeline stages based on actions taken, not manual updates

**Time saved:** ~1-2 hours/day for Glenn, eliminates dead-lead backlog

---

## Phase 3: Operations Layer

**What it solves:** Morgan repeating the same information to the team, order tracking in spreadsheets, documents scattered everywhere, inconsistent processes.

**What gets built:**

- Centralized order/shipment/invoice tracker that syncs automatically -- no more manual spreadsheet updates
- Product collateral system: organized, searchable library of validation reports, info sheets, and spec docs that auto-attaches the right files based on product type and client
- Internal knowledge base with quick-access answers to common team questions -- the stuff Morgan keeps repeating gets written once and served on demand
- Project proposal templates with auto-populated client data so Morgan builds from a foundation instead of from scratch

**Time saved:** ~1-2 hours/day for Morgan, eliminates repeat explanations

---

## Phase 4: Monitoring & Optimization

**What it solves:** Making sure everything keeps running and gets better over time.

**What gets built:**

- Error monitoring and alerting across all automations
- Monthly performance reviews: which sequences convert, where leads stall, what needs tuning
- New automation builds as needs evolve (typically 1-2 per month)
- Priority support for workflow changes and troubleshooting
