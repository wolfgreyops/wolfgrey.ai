What are AI Agents?

Software that works for you, not just with you

An AI agent is software that takes goals, makes decisions, and executes multi-step tasks autonomously. Unlike chatbots that wait for your next prompt, agents act on your behalf.

Think of them as digital employees: you assign work, set boundaries, and they handle execution — checking in only when they need approval or hit an edge case.

  • Autonomous execution — Agents complete tasks end-to-end without hand-holding
  • Multi-agent coordination — Specialized agents work together on complex workflows
  • Human-in-the-loop — You approve key decisions, agents handle the rest
  • Continuous improvement — Agents learn from feedback and get better over time
Why Agents?

From tools you use to teams that work

The shift from chatbots to agents is the shift from asking to delegating.

Chatbots wait

You prompt, they respond, you act. Every step requires your input. You're still doing the work — just with a smarter search bar.

Agents execute

You assign, they deliver. Agents break down goals, make decisions, use tools, and complete multi-step tasks — only checking in when they need you.

Automation is brittle

Traditional automation breaks when inputs change. If X then Y works until Z happens. Then you're debugging flows at 2am.

Agents adapt

Agents reason through edge cases. They handle variations, ask clarifying questions, and find alternative paths when the usual route is blocked.

How It Works

Three things agents need from you

Deploying agents isn't technical. It's about clarity.

Context

What does the agent need to know? Your processes, examples, constraints, and preferences.

Scope

What can the agent decide alone? What needs your approval? Where are the boundaries?

Triggers

When does the agent run? On a schedule? When data changes? When you ask?

Get clear on these three, and deployment is just configuration.

Workflow Types

See how agents work together

Click a workflow type to see how agents coordinate to complete complex tasks.

Linear Workflow

Tasks flow sequentially from one agent to the next. Each agent completes its work before passing to the next stage.

Best for: Lead nurturing, content pipelines, onboarding sequences

Parallel Processing

Multiple agents work simultaneously on different aspects of a task, then results are combined.

Best for: Content creation, proposal generation, competitive analysis

Hierarchical Workflow

Manager agents delegate to specialist agents, who may delegate further. Results roll up through the hierarchy.

Best for: Complex operations, multi-department coordination, enterprise workflows

Event-Driven Workflow

Agents react to events in real-time. When something happens, the appropriate agent is triggered automatically.

Best for: Customer support, notifications, real-time integrations
